What's inside

Play online, at your own pace

Turn-based games against other players: find a rival or invite a friend with your code, then take your turn whenever you like.

Review every move

Every move graded from Brilliant to Blunder, with an accuracy score and an evaluation graph. See the move you missed and play on from there, even offline.

Climb the AI ladder

28 rivals across 7 tiers, from Novice to Champion, each tier ending with a boss. Pick a play style, ask for a hint, or take back a move.

Puzzles every day

200 puzzles across 8 tiers, plus a new daily puzzle with a streak to keep alive. Every puzzle is solved exactly.

Learn to play, then learn to win

An interactive tutorial, the full rules, and 33 strategy lessons, from corners and mobility to the endgame.

Make it yours

32 themes, 24 boards, 24 disc sets, and 100 avatars, earned with coins you win by playing. Dark mode and a High Contrast theme included.

Play anywhere

vs Computer, puzzles, and lessons all work offline, and Pass & Play puts two players on one device. Sign in with Google Play Games to back up your progress.

Ads only when you choose

No banner ads, no ads between games, and no in-app purchases. The only ads are ones you choose to watch, to double the coins you just earned.
