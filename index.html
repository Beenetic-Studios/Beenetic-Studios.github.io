Beenetic Studios is a small independent studio building thoughtful, carefully made games for mobile.

About

Beenetic Studios is the work of Braden Franksen, a solo developer based in the United States. The focus is on classic games rebuilt properly: strong opponents, clean interfaces, and the kind of polish that usually gets cut.

Current project

Reversi Rivals In development

A modern take on Reversi and Othello for mobile, with a deep single-player ladder, online matches against other players, puzzles, and a full review mode that grades every move you make.
